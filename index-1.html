Product

Small decisions that make riders come back

Automatic student fares

Set a registered campus as the destination and the 50% student fare applies. No codes, no forms.

View-once voice notes

Riders and drivers talk in the app. Each voice note plays once, then disappears for good.

Photo pickup

A photo of the gate or the shop sign ends the "where exactly are you?" phone call.

Tap to Pay on iPhone

The driver's phone becomes the card reader. Lower payment costs, fewer cash trips.

A fairer driver fee

17.98% in Saudi Arabia, against roughly 25% at the major platforms.

A fleet that learns

Multi-agent reinforcement learning (MARL) for dispatch, with a first pilot planned in Tabuk.
